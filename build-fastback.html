BLACKLINE PERFORMANCE 1967 FASTBACK GT RICHMOND, VIRGINIA

THE BUILD

An
Icon Refined.

Few cars have the visual presence of a first-generation fastback Mustang. This build was created to preserve that identity while making the car genuinely enjoyable to drive.

The focus is balance. Rather than simply chasing horsepower, the build combines responsive handling, modern braking, improved power delivery, and carefully considered details throughout the vehicle.

The result is a fastback that looks timeless while delivering a driving experience that feels significantly more modern.
